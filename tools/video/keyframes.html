Keyframes v1: 19 frames, timed to the voice-over

Each frame is a still at the moment shown. The notes say how it moves in and out. Every time comes from the recorded voice-over, word by word, so animation can land exactly on the spoken word.

Voice-over 67.7 s · Paul K · ElevenLabs v3 Film ≈ 70.5 s with a 3 s end card 1920 × 1080 Site fonts and colours, no extras

Colours carry meaning

Dark: the problem, and the brand
White: the product at work
Blue: how we work (the site’s process band)
Lime: the call to action, used once, like on the site

Motion rules

  • Everything lands on the spoken word.
  • Text rises out of a mask. Nothing just fades in.
  • Scene changes are wipes at 56°, the angle of the V in the logo.
  • The lime square from the logo is the hero. It swallows the mess, ends the tagline, and becomes the button.
  • Nothing sits still: slow drift, numbers count up, cursors click.
  • Easing: expo-out in, ease-in-out for wipes, a small spring for pops.
